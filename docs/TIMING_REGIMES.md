## The headline finding — timing barely matters for THIS strategy

- Forward 1-year returns are **strong in every regime** (avg ~30–38%, positive 84–94% of the time). There is **no regime where deploying fresh money was a mistake.**
- The biggest surprise: **AVOID (index below its MA / bear) was the SAFEST time to deploy** — 94% of such deployments were positive a year later, with the smallest worst-case (−9%), beating INVEST's 88% / −19%.
- **Why:** the strategy *times itself.* In a downturn few stocks break out, so fresh money sits in **cash** and only deploys into the recovery breakouts; and the **20% trailing stop** caps the damage if you happen to deploy right before a fall. So deploying into fear = a cash cushion plus the rebound — the opposite of what the gut says.

## So what are the four labels good for?

- They describe the **market context** (what phase you're deploying into), not an on/off switch:
- **INVEST / ACCUMULATE** (uptrend) — comfortable to deploy a lump; the book fills fast and rides the trend.
- **HOLD** (overextended / thin breadth) — fine to stay invested; a lump deployed here has the widest range of outcomes (you may be buying a top), so ACCUMULATE in gradually rather than all at once.
- **AVOID** (downtrend) — scary, but historically the **best risk-adjusted** time to deploy, because of the cash + trailing-stop mechanism above. Don't let the red colour stop you; if anything it's the green light for the patient.

## Practical guidance

- **Don't wait for the 'perfect' regime** — idle cash waiting for INVEST cost more than it saved, since every regime paid off within a year. Deploy when you have the money.
- **Run the system continuously.** Gating the strategy's *entries* by a 200-DMA filter **hurt** returns (`REGIME_200DMA.md`); the stop + cash logic already handles downturns.
- **Keep the SIP going always** — ₹20k/month automatically buys more in troughs and less at peaks, so it neutralises almost all remaining timing risk. The regime call only matters for a large one-off lump, and even then AVOID was fine.

## ⚠️ Caveats

- In-sample and survivorship-biased; forward returns are elevated everywhere partly *because* the whole backtest compounds at ~30%. The 40wk MA and breadth are **lagging** and whipsaw in chop, so treat the colour as context, not a trigger. Live, plan for far lower magnitudes.
