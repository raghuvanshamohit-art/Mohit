## Read-out (CAGR & drawdown)

- **Longer look-backs = stronger trend filter.** A 1-year (52w) outperformance gate is the strictest single horizon — it keeps only established leaders, usually the steadiest curve; 3-month (13w) reacts fastest and admits more names.
- **Requiring outperformance over all three horizons (3M & 6M & 1Y)** is the tightest entry screen: fewest, highest-quality names. It tends to give the lowest drawdown but can cost CAGR (you pass on early-stage movers).
- **Adding an outperformance EXIT** on top raises turnover; it can lift CAGR but usually deepens drawdown — watch the trade-off in the table.
- Compare to the plain **regime filter** (index vs its own trend): the outperformance screen (stock vs index) is a *stock-selection* filter, the regime is a *market-timing* one; they reduce drawdown by different mechanisms.

## ⚠️ Caveats

- In-sample, survivorship-biased; the horizon set (13/26/52w) is a fitted choice. Validate out-of-sample before committing to a specific look-back.
