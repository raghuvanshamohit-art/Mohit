## Read-out

- **Bigger positions concentrate the book** (2% ≈ 50 names, 4% ≈ 25, 5% ≈ 20). More concentration usually **raises both CAGR and Max drawdown** — you ride your best names harder but feel the corrections more.
- Watch **Calmar**: if it falls as you size up, the extra return isn't paying for the extra drawdown. The right size is where the drawdown is still one you can actually sit through.
- 2% is the smoothest ride; 4–5% chase more return at the cost of a rougher curve.

## ⚠️ Caveats

- In-sample, survivorship-biased (today's Nifty 500). Concentrated sizing makes results lean harder on a few big winners — more fragile out-of-sample.
