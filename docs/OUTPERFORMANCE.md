## Read-out

- **Requiring Nifty 500 outperformance to enter (relative strength > 0)** keeps you in leaders and out of laggards. It typically trims Max drawdown and lifts risk-adjusted return, at a modest hit to raw CAGR (you take fewer, higher-quality entries).
- **Adding an outperformance EXIT** (sell when a name starts lagging the index) cuts drawdown further but can clip winners during healthy consolidations — watch the CAGR/DD trade-off.
- **Shorter RS lookback (13w)** reacts faster (more trades, tighter) vs 26w (steadier). Neither is 'right' — pick by how much churn you'll tolerate.
- Compare against the plain **regime filter** row: the outperformance (stock-vs-index) and regime (index-vs-its-own-trend) filters attack drawdown from different angles.

## ⚠️ Caveats

- In-sample, survivorship-biased; the RS lookback is a fitted choice — validate out-of-sample before trusting a specific number.
