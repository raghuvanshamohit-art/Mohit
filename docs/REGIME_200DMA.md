## Read-out

- The 200-DMA gate blocks new buys during broad-market downtrends, so it **cuts exposure** and usually **lowers CAGR**; whether it improves drawdown depends on whether it dodges the big declines or just misses the sharp recoveries that follow.
- Because your **20% ratcheting trailing stop already exits losers in a downturn**, the regime gate is largely redundant on the exit side — it mainly throttles re-entry.

## ⚠️ Caveats

- In-sample, survivorship-biased. The 200-DMA is a lagging signal — it flips late at both tops and bottoms, and can whipsaw in choppy years.
