## ⚠️ Why the live result will be LOWER

- **Survivorship bias:** the universe is *today's* Nifty 500 — it never bought the small-caps that delisted, de-rated, or went to zero. This inflates the number the most in exactly the small/mid-cap slice this system favours.
- **In-sample:** parameters were chosen knowing this history; forward performance is systematically worse.
- **Clean-fill assumption:** exits are taken at the stop; real small-caps gap through stops (circuit locks, news) and lose more than 20%.
- **Plan around ~12–15% CAGR**, not this figure; expect 30–45% drawdowns and flat years live.
