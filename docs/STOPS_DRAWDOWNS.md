# Stop-loss hits & drawdowns over the full backtest

> Config: upper Bollinger breakout + Nifty 500 6-month outperformance (26w RS) + **20% ratcheting trailing stop** + 4% sizing, weekly, ₹20L. Window 2008-06-23 → 2026-07-27 (18.1 years). In-sample, survivorship-biased.

## Stop-loss hits

In this system **there is no profit target — every position is eventually closed by the 20% trailing stop.** So 'stop hits' = all exits; the question is whether the stop fired above your entry (locking a gain) or below it (a loss).

| | Count | Share | Avg exit |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| **Total stop hits (all exits)** | 581 | 100% | — |
| Profit-locking (trail above entry) | 295 | 51% | +113% |
| Losing stops (below entry) | 286 | 49% | -16% |
| — near-full ~20% stops (≤ −18%) | 127 | 22% | — |

That's **~32 stop hits per year** (~16 of them losers). Roughly half of all exits actually lock in a profit — the trailing stop is the profit-taker, not just a loss-cutter.

## What to expect operationally

- **~32 exits a year**, about half winners / half losers — a low ~51% hit rate is normal for trend-following; the winners are far bigger than the losers.
- **Small dips are constant** (dozens of ≥5% wobbles) — only a handful reach the ~20–30% range that actually tests your conviction. Those deep ones are the price of the returns.
- Max single drawdown was about the figure in `docs/BACKTEST.md`; expect at least one 20%+ drawdown every few years.

## ⚠️ Caveats

- In-sample, survivorship-biased. Counts scale with position size / filters — this is the 4% + RS config; a 2% book trades more often (more, smaller stop hits).
