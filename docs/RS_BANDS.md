## Read-out

- **RS measures how far the stock has already outrun the index over 6 months.** Higher band = stronger momentum at entry.
- Low bands (0–0.1) are barely-outperforming breakouts; very high bands (>0.6) are rare, so those rows have few trades and low exposure — read them as statistically thin.
- Watch where CAGR peaks vs where drawdown/trade-count stay reasonable: that's the usable momentum zone. A single narrow band throws away most signals (low exposure), so a **floor** (e.g. RS > 0.1 or > 0.2, no upper cap) usually beats any tight band.

## ⚠️ Caveats

- In-sample, survivorship-biased; narrow high-RS bands are thin and fragile. Point-in-time market cap is approximate.
