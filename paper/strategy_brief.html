CW 2σ — Strategy Brief

Compounding Wealth · 2-Sigma · rule-based Indian-equity momentum · weekly

A mechanical system to compound capital by riding winners and cutting losers: buy weekly Bollinger-band breakouts in strong small/mid-caps, size each the same, and let a ratcheting trailing stop decide every exit. No targets, no predictions, no emotions — the rules decide.

1 Universe & scan

  • NSE-listed stocks, market cap ₹1,000–20,000 cr (small & mid-cap — where momentum pays most).
  • Scanned weekly with a screener; decisions are made on the weekly close, never intraday.

2 Entry — all conditions true on the weekly close

  • Breakout: weekly close crosses above the upper Bollinger Band (50, 2σ) — the "2σ" in the name.
  • Relative strength > 0.2 vs Nifty 500 over 26 weeks (the stock is beating the market by >20%):
RS = (Close ÷ Close₂₆w ago) ÷ (N500 ÷ N500₂₆w ago) − 1 > 0.2
  • Market cap within ₹1,000–20,000 cr at entry.
  • Priority when cash/slots are limited: take the highest 26-week RS names first.

3 Exit — one rule only

  • 20% ratcheting trailing stop. Sell when the weekly close falls 20% below the position's highest weekly close since entry.
  • The stop only moves up, never down — it locks in gains as a winner rises and never loosens.
  • No profit targets, no time limit. A winner is held as long as it keeps making new highs; a loser is cut the moment it gives back 20% from its peak.

4 Position sizing

  • Each new buy = 4% of current equity (≈ ₹80,000 today).
  • 25 names × 4% = 100% → the book is fully invested at 25 holdings.
  • Winners are allowed to grow past 4% — you never trim them.

5 Cadence & metrics

  • Weekly: mark holdings to the weekly close, apply stops, scan for new breakouts.
  • Tracked: XIRR (primary, SIP-aware), CAGR, max drawdown, win rate, holding period.

6 Portfolio & cash flow

  • Each week is two independent checks: sell whatever stopped out, then buy new breakouts up to 25 names and available cash. Not forced one-in-one-out.
  • All exit proceeds — profit or loss — go to one cash pool and fund the next breakout at 4% of equity. Cash is cash; the system doesn't treat a winner's proceeds differently from a loser's.
  • SIP ₹20,000 is added on the 1st of each month to the same pool; if the book is full it waits as cash until a slot opens.

✓ The system does

  • Ride winners — let them run, stop ratchets up underneath
  • Cut losers automatically at the 20% trail
  • Recycle every exit into the next breakout
  • Add fresh SIP cash on a fixed schedule

✕ Never does

  • No averaging down / adding to a loser
  • No pyramiding / adding to winners
  • No rebalancing or trimming winners
  • No withdrawing profits, no profit targets

! Reality check

  • Plan for ~12–15% CAGR, not the backtest's 20–30%. The backtest is optimistic — survivorship bias, in-sample fitting, and gap risk all pull live returns lower.
  • Expect 30–45% drawdowns and multi-year flat stretches. A concentrated small/mid-cap book is volatile by design.
  • The edge is discipline, not prediction. The strategy only works if it's followed through the ugly stretches — that's the hard part, and the whole point of paper-trading it first.
