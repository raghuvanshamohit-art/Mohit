## 2. Business Overview

P S Raj Steels is a manufacturer, exporter and importer of **stainless steel products** — pipes, tubes, sheets, coils, bars, angles, strips and plates.

- **Capacity:** ~20,000 MT per annum, plant on ~3 acres in Hisar.
- **Product range:** 250+ standard sizes plus customisation.
- **Markets:** Exports to Gulf, other Asian countries and Africa; imports raw material from Japan and China.
- **Nature of business:** Commodity-linked steel processing — inherently **low-margin, working-capital-heavy**, with profitability tied to stainless-steel spreads and volumes.

**Read-through:**
- **Revenue is flat-to-lumpy** — it peaked at ₹298 Cr in FY24, then settled at ~₹266 Cr for two years. Top line is essentially stagnant (~0% growth TTM).
- **Margins are structurally thin but improving** — OPM has crept up from 3.1% → 4.9% over four years. For a stainless-steel processor this is normal, but it leaves little cushion.
- **Profit is the bright spot** — net profit has grown steadily (₹4 → ₹6 → ₹7 → ₹9 Cr), ~16% profit growth even with flat sales, driven by margin gains and near-zero interest cost.

## 5. Financial Health / Balance Sheet

- **Nearly debt-free:** borrowings of only ~₹1 Cr against reserves of ~₹66 Cr — a genuine strength, especially post-IPO.
- **Total assets** ~₹83 Cr.
- **Returns:** ROCE 16.1% and ROE 12.4% are respectable for the sector and consistent with a low-leverage balance sheet.
- **No dividend** — 0% payout despite consistent profits; capital is being retained.

## 6. Valuation

- **P/E ~41.6** and **P/B ~4.9x** are **rich** for a low-margin, flat-revenue steel processor. Larger, faster-growing steel names typically trade at lower multiples.
- The valuation appears to price in continued margin expansion and growth that the top line has **not yet delivered**.
- The stock sitting **at its 52-week high** after a ~3x run means a lot of optimism is already in the price.

## 8. Strengths

- ✅ **Clean balance sheet** — essentially debt-free with healthy reserves.
- ✅ **Consistent, growing profits** despite flat revenue — evidence of improving operational efficiency.
- ✅ **Decent return ratios** (ROCE 16%, ROE 12%) for the sector.
- ✅ **High promoter holding** (73%+) aligns management with shareholders.
- ✅ **Export footprint** across Gulf, Asia and Africa provides some diversification.

## 9. Risks & Concerns

- ⚠️ **Stagnant top line** — sales have not grown for two years; the growth story rests on margins, not volume.
- ⚠️ **Thin, cyclical margins** — ~5% OPM leaves the business exposed to stainless-steel price swings and input-cost (nickel/chrome/import) volatility.
- ⚠️ **Rich valuation** — P/E ~42 and P/B ~5x are demanding for a commodity processor; little room for disappointment.
- ⚠️ **SME-platform liquidity risk** — NSE Emerge stocks are thinly traded, have large lot sizes, and can be highly volatile (52-week range ₹27.7 → ₹94.8 shows the swing).
- ⚠️ **No dividend** — investors depend entirely on capital appreciation.
- ⚠️ **Small scale & customer/geography concentration** typical of SME manufacturers.

---

## 10. Bottom Line

P S Raj Steels is a **small, well-run, debt-free stainless-steel processor** with steadily rising profits and good return ratios — a fundamentally sound micro-cap. However, the story has two clear tension points: **revenue that has stopped growing**, and a **valuation (P/E ~42, at 52-week highs) that already assumes a lot goes right.** The re-rating from its split-adjusted IPO price has largely happened.

For an investor, the key questions are: *Can it convert flat sales into renewed volume growth, and does the premium multiple survive if margins normalise?* Given SME-platform illiquidity and cyclicality, it suits only investors comfortable with high volatility and a long horizon.
