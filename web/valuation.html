    How to read it. The composite is the median of every model that could run from your inputs, so a single wild estimate can't dominate. The best buy price discounts that composite by your margin of safety. In value mode the ladder steps down below fair value and buys larger tranches the cheaper it gets (the base of the pyramid is widest); in trend mode it steps up from the current price and each add is smaller than the last, with a stop trailing beneath. Models switch off automatically when their inputs are missing (e.g. the Graham Number needs positive EPS and book value; the dividend model needs a dividend).

    Models: Graham Number √(22.5·EPS·BVPS) · Graham revised EPS·(8.5+2g)·4.4/Y · two-stage DCF on FCF/share (or EPS) · earnings power forward-EPS × exit P·E discounted back · Gordon DDM D₁/(r−g). Rates are decimals internally; enter them as percentages.
